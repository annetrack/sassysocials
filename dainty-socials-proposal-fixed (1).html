01 / The opportunity

A polished brand deserves
an equally polished process.

Dainty Socials presents premium digital marketing services across TikTok, Meta, Shopee, content production, and omnichannel strategy. Behind that offer, the inquiry-to-client journey should feel just as organized as the brand itself.

01

Inquiries in different places

Potential clients may arrive through social DMs, forms, referrals, or campaign links — making it easy for conversations to get scattered.

02

Follow-up depends on memory

Without a consistent follow-up process, warm prospects can go quiet before a discovery call or proposal decision.

03

Unclear lead status

It can be difficult to see who is new, booked, qualified, awaiting a proposal, or ready to onboard.

04

Manual admin after the yes

Proposal acceptance, payment, intake, and kickoff can require repeated manual reminders and handoffs.

05

Limited visibility

Without a shared pipeline, it is harder to spot bottlenecks and understand which opportunities need attention.

06

Client experience starts early

A responsive, clear process helps the agency deliver a professional experience before the engagement even begins.

02 / Proposed approach

One clear path from
lead to kickoff.

Set up a centralized CRM and a practical pipeline so every inquiry has a clear next step. Add timely reminders and client onboarding handoffs where they reduce repetitive admin.

Primary objective

Give Dainty Socials a repeatable way to capture and organize prospective clients, manage discovery calls and proposals, and transition confirmed clients into onboarding — without relying on memory or scattered notes.

04 / Example workflow

From first inquiry
to active client.

The following is a proposed workflow to validate with Patricia. Exact stages, triggers, and channels will be finalized during kickoff.

01 · CAPTURE

New inquiry

Contact record created and source recorded where available.

02 · QUALIFY

Discovery call

Booking confirmation, reminders, and outcome tracking.

03 · PROPOSE

Proposal sent

Follow-up task and clear status while the prospect decides.

04 · CONFIRM

Won & payment

Move the opportunity forward after agreed confirmation.

05 · ONBOARD

Kickoff ready

Send intake and kickoff details; create onboarding tasks.

05 / Automation logic

Helpful automations,
with human oversight.

Automations should handle predictable admin while keeping personalized sales conversations in the hands of the agency team.

Prospect experience

  • New inquiry acknowledgement, if the channel supports it
  • Discovery booking confirmation and reminder sequence
  • Internal notification when a qualified inquiry arrives
  • Follow-up task after a completed discovery call
  • Proposal follow-up reminders with manual review before sending

Client handoff

  • Pipeline update when the opportunity is marked won
  • Onboarding checklist and owner assignment
  • Client intake and asset-request email, once approved
  • Internal reminder for payment or kickoff milestones
  • Clear handoff from sales activity to delivery workflow
